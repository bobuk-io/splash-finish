# Splash Finish 0.3.16

- **Antigravity (`agy`) integration:** installs as a self-contained native plugin under `~/.gemini/config/plugins/splash-finish` (honoring `ANTIGRAVITY_CONFIG_DIR`) without editing your own `hooks.json`. Works across Antigravity CLI (`agy`), Antigravity 2.0, and Antigravity IDE. Handles `Stop` (with a bounded local reply preview from `transcriptPath`, error reporting, and silent `USER_CANCELED` clearing), `ask_question` prompts via `PreToolUse`/`PostToolUse`, `PreInvocation` turn-start clearing, and subagent filtering.
- **GitHub Copilot CLI integration:** installs managed skill and hooks (`~/.copilot/hooks/splash-finish.json`, honoring `COPILOT_HOME`), `doctor` status, and **Integrations…** controls, with timestamp-ordered turn-end and permission/input notifications.
- **Codex async questions:** accepted `request_user_input_async` calls exposed through `PostToolUse` now appear as attention cards with the first question title, survive subsequent tool progress, and clear when a user prompt arrives or the session ends.
- **Timed vs. quick Snooze and Unsnooze:** menu bar snooze (**15 Minutes**, **1 Hour**, **Until Tomorrow Morning**) now stays active until its deadline even if the inbox becomes empty or the app restarts, while quick snooze (`Esc`, the Inbox button, or `splash-finish snooze`) wakes on the next new notification or after 15 minutes. While snoozed, the menu bar icon dims (keeping the notification count bright), shows the deadline in its tooltip, and enables **Unsnooze** to resume notifications without opening the inbox.
- Synced shared UI and utilities to BobukKit 0.3.1.

Your inbox, settings, and existing integrations are retained. Run `splash-finish install-skills` (or open **Integrations…**) to install the new Copilot CLI or Antigravity integrations. Homebrew copies update with **Update Splash Finish via Homebrew…**; standalone copies update with **Check for Updates…**.
