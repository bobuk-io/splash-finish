# Splash Finish 0.3.17

- **Color themes across every window:** Settings now offers **System**, **Light** and **Dark** modes plus **18 built-in palettes** (Amber, Sage, Ocean, Terracotta, Catppuccin, Tokyo Night, Kanagawa, Everforest, Rosé Pine, Flexoki, Nord, Dracula, Gruvbox, Solarized, One Light / One Dark, Ayu, Monokai, and GitHub) powered by BobukKit 0.5.0.
- **Full 10-color customization:** adjust **Panel**, **Accent**, and **Text** directly, or expand **More colors** to customize **Surface**, **Muted text**, **Border**, **Selection**, **Link**, **Success**, and **Danger** with per-token **Auto** contrast reset, palette swatches, a live multi-layer preview, and WCAG contrast warnings.
- **Themed Inbox, Settings, and Integrations:** links in messages follow the theme's `link` color, selected cards follow `selection`, and Settings & Integrations windows match the active palette.
- **Settings gear in the Inbox footer:** a gear button in the bottom-right corner of the inbox opens Settings directly.

Your inbox, settings, and existing integrations are retained (older light/dark preferences stay compatible). Homebrew copies update with **Update Splash Finish via Homebrew…**; standalone copies update with **Check for Updates…**.
